Current

  • archeoViz. Data visualization in archaeology . The compagnion blog of the archeoViz application and archeoViz Portal data infrastructure for the re-use, visualization, and dissemination of archaeological spatial data.

Past

  • Préhistoire ancienne en vallée d'Ossau. The page of a collective research project on the Paleolithic and Mesolithic of a Pyrenean valley.
  • Papuan Past Project. The page of a multidisciplinary research project in Papua New Guinea (archaeology, genetics, ethnology).
  • Lectures en sciences sociales . The page of a yearly seminar held at the École française de Rome.
  • Ontologie et connaissance (scientifique) . The companion page of a seminar organized at the EHESS (School for Advanced Studies in the Social Sciences) in 2015.
  • Mission archéologique Franco-Indonésienne à Bornéo . The page of a multidisciplinary research project in Kalimantan (archaeology, genetics, ethnology) (2011–2016)
